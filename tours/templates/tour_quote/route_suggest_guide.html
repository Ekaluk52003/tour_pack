Route Suggest, in pictures

You enter a route. The system finds past quotes with the same cities and nights in the same order, and copies their days whole: hotels, tours and transfers exactly as they were.

1. What counts as a match

A past quote matches when it has the same cities, with the same nights, next to each other in the same order. The system looks for the longest run of your stops that appears in one past quote, so a regular offer such as River Kwai 2 → Ayuthaya 2 → Chiang Mai 3 is found as one piece.

2. How the days are copied

Once a match is found, the days are copied as they are. The system does not pick a tour for each night. If several past quotes match, the pattern that was used most often wins, and the newest one wins a tie.

3. What the user does

Nothing is added to the quote until the user confirms.

4. What the badges mean

BadgeMeaningWhat you do
Copied from quote 26414 · same pattern in 14 of 120 quotes The route was found. The days come from that quote. "14 of 120" says how many of the matching quotes used exactly the same hotels, tours and transfers. Review, then confirm.
No route match - fill in by hand No past quote has this stop next to the stop you asked for. Choose the hotel and services yourself.

Transfers are never copied from a different route

A transfer is copied only when the quote it comes from really had the same route on that side. If a stop was copied from a quote that arrived from, or went on to, a different city, that transfer is left out and an amber note says so. Add it yourself.

No match

Example: you ask for Chiang Mai 9 → Bangkok 9 and no past quote has that. Nothing is guessed, and you choose everything yourself.

  • The green badge names the quote and says how many matching quotes used the same pattern, for example "same pattern in 14 of 120 quotes".

  • Click Back to change the route, or Confirm & fill form to add the days to the quote.
  • If a day has fewer services than expected, a service may have no price for the package type you chose. Try another type.
