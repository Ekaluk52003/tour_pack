Route Suggest, in pictures

You enter a route. The system looks through past quotes for the same two-city legs and suggests the hotels and services they used most.

1. What counts as a match

A past stay is used only when its city and nights are exactly the same as your stop, and the stop beside it matches too. Transfers follow the same rule: the arrival transfer comes only from stays that arrived from the same city, and the departure transfer only from stays that went on to the same next city.

2. How one stop gets its hotel and services

Every matching stay votes. The number you see, such as "25 matching past stays", is how many stays voted.

3. What the user does

Nothing is added to the quote until the user confirms.

4. When the nights are different, or there is no match

The route is what keeps transfers correct, so the system never copies from a different route. It looks for a match in this order and shows a badge for the one it used.

BadgeMeaningWhat you get
Route match · 25 past stays Same city, same nights, and the same neighbouring stop. Hotel and services for every night.
Route match, different nights · 4 past stays Same city and same neighbouring city, but the past stays had a different number of nights. The most-used hotel, the arrival services on the first night (if the city you arrive from matches) and the departure services on the last night (if the city you leave for matches). Middle nights say "fill in by hand".
No route match - fill in by hand No past quote has this stop next to the stop you asked for. Nothing. Choose the hotel and services yourself.

The amber badge: same route, different nights

Example: you ask for Bangkok 3 → River Kwai 2. Past quotes have Bangkok 4 → River Kwai 2, so the nights differ but the route is the same.

  • Hotel: the hotel most of those Bangkok stays used.
  • First Bangkok night: the arrival transfer and services from those stays, as long as they also started the same way (arrived from the same city, or began the trip there).
  • Last Bangkok night: the Bangkok to River Kwai transfer and services, because those stays also went on to River Kwai.
  • Middle night: left empty and marked "fill in by hand". Night 2 of a 4-night stay is not the same as night 2 of a 3-night stay.

The red badge: different route

Example: you ask for Chiang Mai 9 → Bangkok 9, and every past Chiang Mai stay was followed by Phuket. The route is different, so its transfers could be wrong for your trip. Nothing is copied and you choose everything yourself.

Amber and red stops are highlighted in the review step. Check amber stops, especially the hotel and transfers, and fill in the empty nights before you finish the quote.
